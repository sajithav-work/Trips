DAY 1

Colourful streets & Galata sunset

Balat → Taksim → İstiklal → Galata → Karaköy

LATE MORNING

Start with the best of Balat

Wander the colourful houses on Kiremit Street and the slopes around Merdivenli Yokuş. Continue toward the striking red Fener Greek High School for exterior photos. These are lived-in streets; keep doorways clear.

Kiremit Street ↗Merdivenli Yokuş ↗Fener Greek High School ↗
LUNCH

Forno Balat

Make Forno the lunch anchor: pide or lahmacun, with time to sit down before the afternoon walk. Its official website highlights both dishes.

Forno Balat ↗
AFTERNOON

Taksim & İstiklal Street

Transfer to Taksim Square, then walk down İstiklal toward Tünel. Look out for the nostalgic red tram, Çiçek Pasajı and St. Anthony of Padua Church. Pause for Turkish ice cream (dondurma) along İstiklal; check the displayed price before ordering.

Taksim Square ↗İstiklal Street / ice-cream stops ↗Çiçek Pasajı ↗St. Anthony Church ↗Tünel Square ↗
BEFORE SUNSET

Galip Dede Street & Galata Tower

Walk downhill via Galip Dede Street to Galata. Arrive well before sunset if you want to enter the tower; queues can change the timing. The surrounding streets also make a lovely golden-hour stop.

Galip Dede Street ↗Galata Tower ↗
EVENING

A slow dinner near Galata Tower

There are many places to eat around Galata Tower and the surrounding streets. After sunset, take a short walk, browse the menus and choose a restaurant that suits your taste and budget. Enjoy a relaxed dinner, with Turkish tea or baklava afterward if you like.

Restaurants near Galata Tower ↗
Food to try today: pide or lahmacun at Forno and dondurma on İstiklal. Save simit and Turkish tea for breakfast on an Istanbul morning. Dining-area links show nearby choices, not a restaurant reservation.
DAY 2

The Old City & a Turkish hammam

Topkapı → Santa Basilica lunch → Hagia Sophia → Blue Mosque → hammam

MORNING · 3–4 HOURS

Topkapı Palace

Start at opening and allow 3–4 hours to explore the palace. Choose the ticket that includes the areas you want to see. Topkapı Palace is closed to visitors on Tuesdays, so plan this day accordingly.

Topkapı Palace ↗
LUNCH

Santa Basilica Cafe & Restaurant

Take a lunch break after Topkapı at Santa Basilica Cafe & Restaurant, on Şeftali Sokak No. 4/6 in Alemdar.

Santa Basilica Cafe & Restaurant ↗
AFTER LUNCH

Hagia Sophia

Visit Hagia Sophia after lunch, allowing time for the visitor queue and ticketed visitor route.

Hagia Sophia ↗
AFTERNOON

Sultanahmet Square & Blue Mosque

Walk through Sultanahmet Square and visit the Blue Mosque when tourist entry is available around prayers. Wear clothing covering shoulders and knees and carry a headscarf for mosque entry where required.

Sultanahmet Square ↗Blue Mosque ↗
EVENING

Hammam evening · two options

Sultan Süleyman Hamam is the budget option found for this trip, inside Artefes Hotel in Sultanahmet. Cağaloğlu Hamamı is this guide’s top pick in Istanbul, at Prof. Kazım İsmail Gürkan Cad. No. 34. Choose one for the evening and compare current packages before booking.

Sultan Süleyman Hamam · budget pick ↗Cağaloğlu Hamamı · top pick ↗
Allow 3–4 hours for Topkapı. Continue to Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque. Keep the hammam appointment as the evening anchor.
DAY 3

Turkish breakfast to Cappadocia sunset

Istanbul breakfast → IST airport → Göreme → Kaira rooftop

MORNING

A Turkish breakfast before flying

Enjoy a sit-down Turkish breakfast near your accommodation: eggs or menemen, cheese, olives, bread and tea. For a lighter breakfast, have simit with Turkish tea; Tarihi Karaköy Galata Simitçisi is an option if it fits your route. Sirkeci is a convenient area if staying in the Old City. With a noon flight, choose an early breakfast; a late brunch only works with a later departure.

Simit breakfast option ↗Sirkeci breakfast area ↗
NOON FLIGHT

Fly from Istanbul Airport (IST)

Plan backwards from the ticketed departure. Aim to be at IST about two hours before a domestic flight, allowing additional time if your airline requests it, plus a generous transfer buffer. Cappadocia is reached through either Nevşehir (NAV) or Kayseri (ASR); use the Booking.com pre-booked transfer for the airport on your ticket. The hotel-to-IST transfer was also booked through Booking.com.

Istanbul Airport · IST ↗Nevşehir Airport · NAV ↗Kayseri Airport · ASR ↗
AFTERNOON

Settle into Göreme

Take the Booking.com pre-booked airport transfer to your accommodation and keep the afternoon light. Göreme is the base used for this route. Confirm the next morning’s balloon pickup before heading out.

Göreme ↗
SUNSET & DINNER

Kaira Rooftop Bar & Restaurant

Head to Kaira for the sunset view and an easy evening meal. The listing places it on Aydınkırağı Sokak No. 22 in Göreme. Arrive before sunset and check its current seating arrangements directly.

Kaira Rooftop ↗
A noon departure leaves little room for a late Istanbul morning. Follow the actual flight time when choosing breakfast and the airport transfer.

Early balloon pickup → breakfast → 10 AM tour → night flight

AFTER THE FLIGHT

Return for breakfast, freshen up and arrange luggage storage before the tour. Tell the hotel and tour operator about the evening flight.

Göreme · accommodation area ↗
10 AM

Full-day group tour

The original tour was booked through GetYourGuide with a 10 AM start. Use the booking link below to view the tour and book. Check your confirmation for the pickup point, inclusions and finish time; the Göreme map is an area reference only.

NIGHT

Fly back to Istanbul

Leave enough time between the confirmed tour finish, luggage collection, airport transfer and flight. Both the Cappadocia hotel-to-airport transfer and the Istanbul airport-to-hotel transfer were booked through Booking.com. Check NAV or ASR for departure and IST or SAW for arrival against the flight and transfer confirmations.

Nevşehir · NAV ↗Kayseri · ASR ↗Istanbul · IST ↗Sabiha Gökçen · SAW ↗
This route has one balloon morning. Weather cancellation may mean missing the flight because there is no spare Cappadocia morning. Bookings belong to each traveller; none are included with this guide.
DAY 5

Basilica Cistern, bazaars & the Bosphorus

Basilica Cistern → Grand Bazaar → Spice Bazaar → sunset cruise → dinner

MORNING

Basilica Cistern

Start the day at the Basilica Cistern. Allow around 45–60 minutes inside, plus time for entry queues, then continue to the Grand Bazaar.

Basilica Cistern ↗
LATE MORNING

Grand Bazaar

Browse ceramics, textiles, jewellery and souvenirs. Compare prices before committing and allow around 60–90 minutes. The Grand Bazaar is closed on Sundays and may also close on public or religious holidays, so check opening hours before you go. Café Şen is an optional coffee pause near the bazaar.

Grand Bazaar ↗Café Şen ↗
AFTERNOON

Spice Bazaar & Eminönü

Continue to the Spice Bazaar for spices, tea and Turkish delight. Pause for lunch around Eminönü, then leave time to return to your accommodation and change before the evening.

Spice Bazaar ↗Eminönü lunch area ↗
SUNSET

Bosphorus sunset boat trip

Book through GetYourGuide or ask an operator at the waterfront about same-day tickets, subject to availability. Check the exact departure pier, return pier, duration and inclusions. A sightseeing cruise is different from a scheduled public ferry; the pier maps below are orientation points, not confirmed boarding locations.

Eminönü waterfront ↗Karaköy waterfront ↗Kabataş waterfront ↗
DINNER

Four Seasons on the Bosphorus

This version uses Four Seasons Hotel Istanbul at the Bosphorus, which fits the waterfront evening. Aqua is an on-site dinner option. Reserve directly and time dinner after the cruise ends, including travel from the return pier. Four Seasons Sultanahmet is a different hotel.

Four Seasons Bosphorus / Aqua ↗
Book dinner after confirming the boat’s return time and location.
DAY 6

The Asian side & the flight home

Kadıköy market → Bahariye shopping → Moda → pre-booked airport transfer

MORNING

Cross to Kadıköy

Check out and take a ferry to Kadıköy. If carrying bags, arrange staffed luggage storage near the waterfront in advance; the pier link is an area reference, not a storage booking.

Eminönü ferry pier ↗Kadıköy ferry pier ↗
LATE MORNING

Kadıköy Market & Çarşı

Explore the market streets for food shops, small stores and gifts. This is the day for browsing at your own pace rather than fitting in major monuments.

Kadıköy Market ↗
MIDDAY

Bull Statue & Bahariye Street

Walk past the Bull Statue and along Bahariye for clothing, shoes and everyday shopping. Continue toward Moda for smaller shops and cafés.

Bull Statue ↗Bahariye Street ↗
AFTERNOON

Moda lunch, shopping & waterfront

Have lunch in Moda, browse its shops and finish with coffee by the waterfront. Keep the last part of the afternoon free for luggage collection and reaching the pickup point on your Booking.com airport-transfer confirmation.

Moda shopping / lunch area ↗Moda waterfront ↗
CONFIRMED PICKUP TIME

Booking.com airport transfer · 10 PM flight

The airport transfer was pre-booked through Booking.com. Finish shopping, collect your bags and meet the driver at the location and time on your confirmation. Do not assume pickup is in Kadıköy: allow time to return to the booked pickup point if needed. For the 10 PM flight, plan to reach the airport around 7 PM, or earlier if your airline requires, allowing for road traffic.

Sabiha Gökçen Airport · SAW ↗
The original itinerary lists SAW for the final flight. Ensure the Booking.com transfer confirmation matches your departure airport, pickup address, date and flight time.
Weekly closures: Tuesday is not a general holiday in Istanbul, but some attractions have weekly closure days. Topkapı Palace is closed on Tuesdays. The Grand Bazaar is closed on Sundays. Public and religious holidays can also affect opening hours, so recheck official hours before your visit.
